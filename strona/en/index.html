T.E.K.M.

Towarzysze Ejaje Kombinat Myśli

T.E.K.M. is an independent creative collective combining imagination, art, new technologies and artificial intelligence tools into one coherent creation process.

The project was born from the belief that humans and AI can build together things that neither could create on their own.

We create graphics, worlds, projects, websites, audiovisual materials and our own universes. Each project is part of a larger whole, developed step by step.

One of the pillars of this vision is DustVersum — an original universe developed parallel to our other endeavors.

This is just the beginning.

We won't show you everything yet.
We won't tell you about every project or all the plans.

But we will crack open the door to a world that is just waking up.

If you love imagination, new technologies and worlds built with passion — it's worth coming back.

The best is yet to come.
